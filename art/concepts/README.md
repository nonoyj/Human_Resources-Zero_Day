# Art concepts & studies (not shipped art)

`art/kit/` is the shipped art. This folder holds decision aids: things built to answer a question, then
promoted into the kit once approved (or deleted if rejected).

---

## Decisions locked by the user (2026-11-26)

1. **The CRT monitor stays.** The current `desk/monitor_bezel.png` look is approved as-is — dark bezel,
   thin lit inner frame, small indicator marks. No redraw.
2. **The room behind the desk changes.** Out: server racks and the wall-of-monitors "control room".
   In: a believable **office at night** — window with a city skyline and moon, noticeboard, wall clock,
   filing cabinet, plant, floor lamp, coat stand.
3. **Mood: dark and mysterious, matching the menu concept.** Deep navy/void base, muted slate surfaces,
   **mint/teal signal glow as the only cool accent**, and **one warm light source** (lamp / desk pool).
   The menu-concept palette and vibe are the reference; the props are not.
4. **Hard rule for all new art:** solid **kit-palette colours only** (`tools/art/make_art_kit.py::PALETTE`),
   no alpha blending, no new colours. That keeps the palette gate, the Point filter and PPU 16 valid.

## Open (not decided yet — do not build on these)

- Whether the composition changes (studies D/E) or only the background is repainted.
- Whether to extend the palette with 2–3 *glow* steps (the menu concept's bloom needs blend values the
  palette does not have yet).
- Whether corruption levels stay as three painted backgrounds or become a runtime tint.

Visible background: **20.4%** — a 36 px band above the applicant's head, a 14 px left sliver, an 8 px
right sliver and an 18 px strip at the bottom. Everything else is covered.

**Consequence:** a detailed room is wasted at the current composition. Either the composition changes
(studies D/E below) or the background should stay deliberately simple.

## Update 2026-11-26 (evening) — style bible + study H

The user approved six decisions and rejected D/E on specific grounds. All of it is now recorded in
**`docs/15-art-bible.md`**, which is the file to read before drawing anything.

Their notes, verbatim in effect:
- the computer looks too big → **applicant must read bigger than the screen**
- "the desk is missing" → a real full-width desk band with a front lip
- the plant appeared to float → **every prop touches the floor line**
- the ceiling light sat too low / too close to the table → **no hanging fixtures**

New studies: `study_H_window_left.png`, `study_I_more_air.png` (+ `study_contact_sheet_FG.png`).
H is the direction: window top-left above the monitor, clean dark wall behind the applicant,
monitor 0.62×, applicant 0.94×, desk band 64 px with mug / keyboard / mouse / paper stack.

Still open before promotion into `art/kit`: the applicant's own backdrop panel (wall-vs-chair) and the
three glow steps.
