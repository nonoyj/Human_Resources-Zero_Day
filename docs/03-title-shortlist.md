# Title Shortlist

Criteria used: (1) instantly communicates "you judge who is human", (2) reads as a
cyber/tech product name, (3) short enough for a launcher icon + store title (≤ 22 chars
for the main wordmark), (4) low collision with existing games, (5) no trademark-adjacent
wording from *Papers, Please* or *That's Not My Neighbor*.

| # | Title | Tagline | Why it works | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **HUMAN RESOURCES: ZERO DAY** | *Not every applicant is a person.* | Double meaning (HR dept / human as a resource), "zero day" = unpatched vulnerability = the AI you let in | Long wordmark; needs a compact icon version (**HR//0D**) |
| 2 | **VERIFIED HUMAN** | *Prove it.* | Mirrors the CAPTCHA phrase every player already knows; ironic because humans must prove it | Slightly generic |
| 3 | **APPLICANT ZERO** | *The first one always gets in.* | Implies patient-zero infiltration; short, filmic, good logo | Obscures the HR framing |
| 4 | **SILICON GATE** | *Guard the door between humans and machines.* | Gatekeeping metaphor + hardware pun | Abstract, no HR signal |
| 5 | **ZERO-DAY HIRE** | *Fast. Cheap. Tireless. Not human.* | Cyber term + hiring term in 3 words | Reads as a job-board ad if seen alone |
| 6 | **LAST HUMAN HIRED** | *You are the firewall.* | Emotional, ties to the job-displacement theme | Longer, sadder tone |
| 7 | **TRUSTED SOURCE** | *Everyone claims to be one.* | Real security jargon (phishing sender trust) | Too insider for non-technical players |
| 8 | **SYNTHETIC STAFF** | *They never take a day off.* | Creepy-corporate, memorable | Two-word generic sci-fi feel |
| 9 | **CLEARANCE: LEVEL HUMAN** | *One stamp decides who gets in.* | Genre nod + identity theme | Slightly clunky |
| 10 | **FIREWALL OF FLESH** | *The last line is a person.* | Very distinct, thematic | Too weird for an awareness/comms product |

## Recommendation

**`HUMAN RESOURCES: ZERO DAY`**

- Wordmark: `HUMAN` (bold, monospaced, terminal green) / `RESOURCES` (thin, letter-spaced)
  stacked, with `ZERO DAY` as a red sub-label — reads as a corporate badge.
- Icon version: **HR//0D** stencil on a dark card with a scan-line.
- Filename-safe slug: `human-resources-zero-day`, package id `com.<studio>.hrzeroday`.

## Runner-up (if you want a shorter, punchier name)

**`VERIFIED HUMAN`** — best for marketing and public-awareness framing; the tagline does the
explaining. The two can coexist: *VERIFIED HUMAN* as the public-facing title,
*HUMAN RESOURCES: ZERO DAY* as the in-game "world" branding on the intro screen.
